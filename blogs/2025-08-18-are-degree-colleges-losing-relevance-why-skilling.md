The changing face of education in India For decades, Indian families placed immense faith in a college degree, viewing it as the surest path to a good job. Parents would invest lakhs in traditional colleges, hoping their children would secure stable careers — and by extension, secure lives. In many households, a degree was considered essential not only for employment, but also for eligibility.
