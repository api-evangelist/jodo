Picture this: It’s 2007, and a young professional is doubled over in pain from recurring kidney stones. Fast forward to 2025, and that same person has built a thriving coaching business with nearly 20,000 community members worldwide. This isn’t just another success story—it’s a window into India’s personal coaching revolution, where traditional boundaries between teacher and student, offline and online, ancient wisdom and modern delivery are being rewritten entirely.
