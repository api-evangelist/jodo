Running a modern educational institution is becoming increasingly complex. From fee collection and attendance to examinations, academics, transport, communication, and compliance — schools, colleges, and universities juggle dozens of administrative tasks every day. A Campus Management System (CMS) solves this problem by centralising all academic and administrative functions into an integrated digital platform.
