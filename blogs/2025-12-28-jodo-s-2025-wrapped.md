Jodo is almost six years old now. What started as a mission to turn education payments into something as easy as setting up your Hotstar–Netflix subscription had a big year in 2025. As of December 2025 , Jodo works with 5,000+ institutes across India — schools, colleges, universities, coaching centres, and even personal coaches who run their business from WhatsApp.
