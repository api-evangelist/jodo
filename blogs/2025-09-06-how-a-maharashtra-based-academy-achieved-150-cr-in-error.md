About the institution This leading Maharashtra-based coaching institute has built a reputation for academic excellence and large-scale competitive test prep. With thousands of students enrolling across programs each year, managing fee collection had become a critical operational challenge - especially with multiple fee components and payment modes. Institute’s fee collection challenge The institution’s fee structure was complex where different fee components (such as tuition, exam fees, transport, and extracurriculars) had to be credited to different bank accounts.
